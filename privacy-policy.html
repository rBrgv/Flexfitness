Flex Fitness — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Flex Fitness Gym ("we", "us") operates a WhatsApp-based assistant to help members and visitors with questions about our gym, and a web form used to gather feedback from members and staff. This page explains what information we collect through these channels and how it's used.

WhatsApp Assistant

When you message our WhatsApp number, we receive and store:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • The content of the messages you send us
  • Our assistant's replies to you

Messages are processed using Anthropic's Claude API to generate helpful replies about our gym's hours, services, and classes. Certain messages (complaints, injuries, medical questions, or anything our assistant can't confidently answer) are flagged and shared with gym staff so a person can follow up directly.

We do not sell or share your WhatsApp phone number or message content with third parties for marketing purposes. Your conversation is used only to respond to you and, where relevant, to improve how we run the gym.

Discovery Feedback Form

Our discovery form collects your role at the gym, your name (optional depending on role), and your written answers to questions about your experience with Flex Fitness. This information is stored securely and used internally to improve our services and systems. It is not shared publicly or sold to third parties.

Data Storage

Form responses are stored in a Supabase database with access restricted by password. WhatsApp conversation data is processed to generate assistant replies and is not retained longer than necessary to provide this service.

Your Rights

You can ask us to delete your data, or ask what data we hold about you, at any time by contacting the gym directly.

Contact

For any questions about this policy or your data, please contact Flex Fitness Gym directly at the front desk or via WhatsApp.
